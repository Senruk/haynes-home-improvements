# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Homeowners within about 200 miles of Bedford (London, Birmingham, Manchester and between) planning bespoke built-in furniture: bedrooms, fitted wardrobes, dressing rooms, media walls, loft/eaves storage, home offices and kitchens. Usually browsing on a phone in the evening after a friend's recommendation or a search, comparing a few local fitters. They want proof of finish quality, a sense of who turns up, and an easy way to ask for a quote.

## Product Purpose
Marketing site for Haynes Home Improvements Ltd ("Bespoke Design and Build"), a fitted-furniture business based in Bedford (office in London) that covers up to about 200 miles. Its job is to show real work, make the coverage clear, and turn visitors into quote requests, calls and WhatsApp messages. Success = enquiries through the quote form or phone.

## Brand Personality
Solid, house-proud, straight-talking. Calm confidence of a tradesperson whose work speaks for itself. Warmth comes from the light sage-and-gold palette and the photography, not from salesy copy.

## Anti-references
- Generic trade-directory templates (stock-photo hero, blue/orange, "Welcome to our website").
- Flat-pack retailer look (bright, promotional, discount-led).
- Over-styled luxury interiors sites that hide the actual work behind mood shots.

## Design Principles
1. The work is the hero: real project photos lead, copy supports.
2. Speak plainly: say what gets built, where, and how to get a quote.
3. Brand-true: a lighter sage green with gold accents and gold buttons, on a light, clean page.
4. Specific and honest: up to 200 miles from the Bedford base, shown on the map, never "nationwide"; never claim facts the client hasn't confirmed (reviews, timescales, guarantees, in-house manufacture).

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA. Keyboard-usable gallery and lightbox, visible focus, reduced-motion respected, descriptive alt text for every project photo.
