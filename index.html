How it works

Four steps from first visit to finished room.

  1. 1

    We visit and measure

    We come to you, look at the space and talk through what you need to store.

  2. 2

    We design and quote

    A layout drawn to your measurements, with finishes and a clear price up front.

  3. 3

    We build it

    Cabinets, doors and drawers cut to size for your room, in the finish you chose.

  4. 4

    We fit it and tidy up

    We install, trim everything to fit your walls and ceiling exactly, and leave the room clean.
