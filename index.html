About Us

London's trusted name in bespoke joinery

Haynes Home Improvements Ltd brings years of expertise to every project. Based near Great Portland Street in central London, we specialise in bespoke fitted wardrobes, custom kitchens, and high-end interior joinery that transforms spaces.

Every project begins with a conversation, understanding how you live, what you need, and the details that matter to you. From initial design through to installation, we manage every stage in-house, keeping every detail on track and the finish exactly as promised.

With a 5.0 Google rating and a growing portfolio of happy clients across London, our reputation is built on one thing: delivering exactly what we promise, beautifully crafted.
